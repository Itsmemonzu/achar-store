Payments

Pay the rider in cash, or pay online. Online payments go through SSLCOMMERZ, Bangladesh's payment gateway, which accepts cards, mobile banking and internet banking.

This is a demo storefront. The SSLCOMMERZ checkout shown here is a visual preview only. It is not connected to SSLCOMMERZ, no payment is processed, and you should never enter real card or wallet details.

Exchanges

Exchange any unworn Line 01 piece for another size within 7 days. Inside Dhaka, we pick it up from your door. Outside Dhaka, send it back with the courier and we'll ship the new one free.

Size guide

Line 01 is cut boxy. Prefer it closer? Take one size down. Inches, garment laid flat (sample chart).

FAQ

Can I open the box before paying?

Yes. With cash on delivery you can check the shirt in front of the rider before you pay.

Is paying online safe?

On the live store, online payments are handled on SSLCOMMERZ's secure page. Northline never sees or stores your card number.

Do you ship outside Bangladesh?

Not yet. Going global? Hopefully soon.
